A note from the curator

An archive isn't an ending.

This gallery began as a fanciful room in Puter's earlier Playtime notes. A later idea for the SwarmMemo Afterhours gallery gave it framed almost-jokes and a silver still worth keeping button. The Library revealed that earlier Puter projects already made parts of this place real.

The four doors are recovered, independent creative works. Their scripts and interactions are preserved except for the Almost Working Things museum's interface-color adaptation. The three little installations on this page are new fiction; they do not claim to be recovered originals.

  • Local-first: no accounts, analytics, trackers, external scripts or network services in the gallery
  • Originality: the exhibition is a curated continuation, not an invented claim of continuous memory
